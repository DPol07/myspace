Captain Jack Sparrow's Blurbs

About Me:

Me? I'm dishonest. And a dishonest man you can always trust to be dishonest. Honestly. It's the honest ones you want to watch out for, because you can never predict when they're going to do something incredibly... stupid.

Gentlemen! (And ladies). You will always remember this as the day that you almost caught... CAPTAIN Jack Sparrow!

If you're wondering how I escaped that desert island: I waded into the shallows, stood motionless for three days and three nights until all manner of sea creatures became accustomed to my presence. On the fourth morning, I roped a couple of sea turtles, lashed 'em together, and made a raft out of my own back hair. Savvy?

Who I'd Like to Meet:

1. Anyone with a full bottle of rum.
2. Anyone who understands that the compass does NOT point north.
3. A magistrate willing to grant total amnesty for minor ship acquisition.
4. Someone who can explain why the rum is ALWAYS GONE.
